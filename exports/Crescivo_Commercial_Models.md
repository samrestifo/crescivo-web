## 1. Growth Diagnostic™ (only)

**The entry point. Fixed fee. Three weeks. Board-ready Revenue Architecture™ blueprint.**

- **Structure:** fixed-fee, fixed-scope, time-boxed to 3 weeks.
- **What it does:** maps the constraints, dependencies and opportunities inside your current
  commercial motion, then translates them into a board-ready Revenue Architecture™ blueprint.
- **Outputs:** Founder Dependency Score, Revenue Architecture Review, ICP Clarity Assessment,
  Positioning & Messaging Audit, GTM Motion Review, Sales Process Review, Pipeline Health Score,
  Forecast Confidence Review, Partner Ecosystem Map, Leadership Cadence Review, Team Capability
  Assessment, KPI Framework Design, 90-Day Revenue Blueprint.
- **Commitment:** none beyond the diagnostic. No retainer required to start.
- **Indicative fee:** `[FIXED DIAGNOSTIC FEE]`

**Use when:** the board or leadership wants an independent, structured read on where growth is
founder-dependent — and a credible plan — before committing to an ongoing engagement.

---

## 2. Embedded Operator Retainer

**Embedded execution, not advisory deliverables.**

- **Structure:** monthly retainer.
- **Cadence:** weekly operating rhythm with your leadership team.
- **What it does:** installs the revenue operating rhythm — pipeline governance, leadership cadence,
  partner and GTM execution — and runs it inside the business.
- **Reporting:** monthly board-ready revenue visibility (pipeline, forecast confidence, partner
  leverage, growth constraints).
- **Indicative fee:** `[MONTHLY RETAINER]` · minimum term `[e.g. 3–6 months]`

**Use when:** the architecture is already understood and the priority is execution, cadence and
turning strategy into managed, repeatable growth.

---

## 3. Diagnostic + Embedded Execution

**The default path. Diagnose, then build.**

- **Structure:** fixed diagnostic fee, then a monthly embedded engagement.
- **Flow:** Growth Diagnostic™ (3 weeks) → board-ready blueprint → embedded execution against it.
- **Why:** the diagnostic de-risks the engagement and scopes the retainer precisely; the embedded
  phase turns the blueprint into operating cadence and founder autonomy.
- **Indicative fee:** `[FIXED DIAGNOSTIC FEE]` then `[MONTHLY RETAINER]`

**Use when:** you want clarity first and execution second — the most common and recommended route.

---

## 4. Success Fee Model

**Lower fixed cost. Upside tied to outcomes.**

- **Structure:** lower monthly retainer, plus a success fee.
- **Upside tied to (select):** new or incremental revenue · ARR growth · partner-sourced pipeline ·
  signed contracts · other agreed commercial milestones.
- **What it signals:** commercial alignment — Crescivo shares in the outcome it is engaged to create.
- **Indicative terms:** retainer `[LOWER MONTHLY RETAINER]` + success fee `[% / $ basis and trigger]`

**Use when:** the client prefers lower fixed cost and fees aligned to measurable revenue outcomes,
and the metrics can be cleanly attributed.

---

## 5. Equity / Strategic Advisory Model

**Selective only.**

- **Structure:** lower cash fee plus equity participation.
- **Involvement:** board and/or advisory engagement alongside the operating work.
- **Selectivity:** offered only where there is high conviction, strong founder alignment and a
  long-horizon partnership thesis.
- **Indicative terms:** cash `[REDUCED FEE]` + equity `[% / instrument / vesting]`

**Use when:** both sides want deep, long-term alignment and the relationship justifies equity
participation rather than a pure fee-for-service arrangement.

---

## 6. Hybrid Model

**Tailored blend of certainty and alignment.**

- **Structure:** diagnostic fee + monthly retainer + success fee, with an optional equity component.
- **What it does:** combines the certainty of a fixed diagnostic, the cadence of an embedded
  retainer, and the alignment of success/equity upside — calibrated to the client's stage and risk
  appetite.
- **Indicative terms:** `[DIAGNOSTIC FEE]` + `[MONTHLY RETAINER]` + `[SUCCESS FEE]` + `[OPTIONAL EQUITY]`

**Use when:** a single standard model doesn't fit and you want the engagement shaped to the specific
commercial context.

---

## How to choose

1. **Start with the Growth Diagnostic™** unless the architecture is already well understood.
2. **Match cadence to the constraint** — execution gaps point to an embedded retainer; clarity gaps
   point to the diagnostic.
3. **Use success/equity for alignment** where outcomes are attributable or the partnership is
   long-horizon.
4. **Keep it board-ready** — every model is designed to produce clear executive reporting and a
   defined path to founder autonomy and repeatable enterprise growth.
